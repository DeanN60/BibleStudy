The rapture of Philip

Immediately after baptizing the Ethiopian eunuch, the Spirit of the Lord "caught away" (harpazō (G726)) Philip. Rather than being taken to heaven, Philip was supernaturally transported across land to the city of Azotus.

This is interesting because Philip suddenly disappeared and was teleported to another location on the earth. In this case the word harpazō (G726) was used to describe Philip being teleported from one location on the earth to another location.
