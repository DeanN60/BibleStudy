Theology is the study of God. "Theos" is the word for God. "Ology" means study of.

Types of Theology

  • Covenant Theology
  • Dispensational Theology
  • Historical Theology
  • Reformed Theology
  • Systematic Theology

For the most part each of the Theologies are compatible with each other. In a few cases, there may be differences in understanding.

Additional Studies

  • Ecclesiology - Study of Church History
  • Eschatology - Study of End Times
  • Israelology - Study of Israel
