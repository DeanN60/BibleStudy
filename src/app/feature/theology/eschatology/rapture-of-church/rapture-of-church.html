is an Old Testament passage describing the rising of the dead. The risen will be told to enter their chambers and close the door until the indignation is past. Verse 21 explains that the indignation is when God comes out of His place to punish the earth. Note the order of sequence here. It is all before the indignation and they are commanded to stay there until the indignation has past.

  1. Rise from Dead
  2. Enter Chamber and Close the Door
  3. Stay in Chamber until the Indignation has past
