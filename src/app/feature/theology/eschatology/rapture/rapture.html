Harpazō (G726) means to be snatched up or caught up by force.

says that the Lord Himself will comedown from heaven with a loud command. He will gather up the dead in Christ and then those in Christ who are living will be gathered.

says that we shall not all sleep, but in the twinkling of the eye we shall be changed into an incorruptible body. Notice that our body will be transformed in the twinkling of the eye and we shall be changed. It does not say that we will disappear in the twinkling of the eye.
