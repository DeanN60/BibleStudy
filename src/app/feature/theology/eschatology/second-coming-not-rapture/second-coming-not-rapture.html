Not the Rapture because Jesus Christ physically appears and sets His feet on the earth. Jesus comes to defeat the antichrist.

Feature

Christ physically returns to Earth with His saints

Location

says that He will stand on the Mount of Olives. The Mount of Olives will split and a large vally will cause the mountain to split North and South.

Bodily Return

says that Jesus will physically return in bodily form.

Purpose

says that Jesus will defeat the antichrist with His breath.

says that Jesus will through the antichrist and his prophet into the lake of fire and brimstone.

Visibility

  universal; every eye will see Him.

Timing

says that Jesus will gather all of the nations of the earth against Israel. Then He will go forth and save Israel.
