The rapture of Elijah

Elijah was taken up into heaven in a whirlwind, separated from Elisha by a chariot and horses of fire, leaving the earth without dying.

In this example, we clearly see Elijah was taken up visibly to heaven. The text only mentions that the mantle he was wearing was dropped off for Elisha to pick up. It says nothing about Elijah's clothes falling off.

The text states that Elijah was taken up in a chariot of fire with horses of fire. The chariot and horses separated Elijah and Elisha.
