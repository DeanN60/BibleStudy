The five solas of the reformation. It is these solas which differentiate the reformers from the teachings of Rome.

  1. Sola Scriptura – By Scripture alone

    says that all Scripture is God breathed. This means that God is the author of Scripture.

  2. Sola Fide – By faith alone

    says that salvation is by faith alone and not by any works.

    says that we are justified by faith apart from the works of the law.

    says that we trust God for our faith.

  3. Sola Gratia – By grace alone

    says that it is by grace alone.

    says that the kindness and affection of God our Savior saved us by His grace.

  4. Solus Christus – Through Christ alone

    says that Jesus is the only way to the father.

    says that God has given us eternal life through His son Jesus.

  5. Soli Deo Gloria – Glory to God alone

    says that God will not share His glory with another.

    says that He will not give His glory to anyone else.
