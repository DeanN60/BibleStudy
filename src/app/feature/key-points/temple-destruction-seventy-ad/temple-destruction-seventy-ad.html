Many assume that the Roman Legions who destroyed the temple in Jerusalem were all Italians. A few were Italians, but Rome took soldiers from the local area to populate the legions.

What nationality was the make up of the Roman legions who destroyed the temple in 70AD?

Four Roman legions—Legio X Fretensis, Legio V Macedonica, Legio XV Apollinaris, and Legio XII Fulminata—took part in the siege and destruction of the Second Temple in 70AD, drawing their soldiers from provincial regions across the Roman Empire rather than the city of Rome itself.
